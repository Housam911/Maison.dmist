How to wear it

The ritual

01

Start warm

Mist over clean, just-dry skin. Warmth opens the scent and helps it settle rather than sit on the surface.

02

Keep your distance

Hold the bottle about twenty centimetres away and sweep slowly — shoulders, collarbone, the inside of the wrists.

03

Layer, don't drench

Two or three passes are plenty. Add another later in the day when you want the trail back.

The house

Maison d'Mist

Maison d'Mist began with a simple wish: a scent light enough to wear from morning to night, and soft enough that it feels like it belongs to your skin rather than sitting on top of it.

Every mist is composed around that idea. Nothing heavy, nothing loud — only the kind of fragrance you reach for without thinking, and that people notice only when they come close.
