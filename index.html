How to apply it

The ritual

01

Start warm

Apply to clean, dry skin. Warmth helps the texture melt in and the scent come through.

02

Find the right touch

Smooth the lotion in, or let the butter melt between your palms, then apply to the shoulders, collarbone and the inside of the wrists.

03

Layer, don't overdo it

A little is enough. Add more later in the day when you want the comfort and the scent back.
